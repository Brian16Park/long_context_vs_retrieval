## The run

- 81,000 answered calls: long context 7,200, RAG 72,000, closed book 1,800; 3 failed attempts, all retried.

- Replies cut off by the 400-token cap: long context 27, RAG 4, closed book 5.
- Caching worked as the cost model assumes: 465 of 470 corpora were written to the cache once and read after that. 5 were written twice, after the run was interrupted (about $0.32 extra).
